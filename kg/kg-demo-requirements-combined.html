Step 01 — Two Different Documents, One Graph

Requirements vs Test Plan — both needed

This Document — Requirements (LTESMS REQ)

Defines what the device must do. Each requirement is a mandatory obligation. Written in "shall / should / may" language.

  • Device shall support SMS over IMS
  • Device shall retry MO SMS after 30 seconds
  • Device shall support 3GPP and 3GPP2 formats
  • Device shall support Text-to-988 (FCC mandate)
  • Device shall support GSM-7bit and UCS-2 encodings
  • Device shall send SIP 200 OK on MT SMS receipt

Previous Document — Test Plan (LTESMS TC)

Defines how to verify those requirements. Each test case maps to one or more requirements and has procedures + expected results.

  • TC-6306: verifies MT SMS over IMS
  • TC-6323: verifies SIP retry timer behavior
  • TC-6363: verifies GSM-7bit encoding
  • TC-7349: verifies Text-to-988 capability
  • TC-6306 → maps to REQ-29575, REQ-29578
  • Together they form a complete traceability chain

A knowledge graph can hold both — linking requirements to tests to outcomes in a single queryable structure.

Step 02 — The Raw Requirements Text

What the document actually says

REQ VZ_REQ_LTESMS_29549 — Retry Mechanism (Initial Attempt)
The device makes an initial attempt to send the MO SMS. The initial attempt is considered to have failed when the SIP timer (Timer F) expires.

REQ VZ_REQ_LTESMS_29550 — Retry Mechanism (Second Attempt)
The device shall support a retry mechanism where it waits 30 seconds and then performs a second attempt to send the MO SMS.

REQ VZ_REQ_LTESMS_29551 — TP-MR Preservation
If the MO SMS is in 3GPP format, the device shall in the second attempt use the same TP-Message-Reference (TP-MR) as in the initial attempt.

REQ VZ_REQ_LTESMS_4105999311951475 — Text-to-988 (FCC Mandate)
The device shall be able to send/receive SMS to/from 988 (Suicide Prevention Lifeline) over IMS. This is a mandatory requirement by FCC-21-119.

Every sentence is a potential triple. Every "shall" is a directed relationship waiting to be mapped.
